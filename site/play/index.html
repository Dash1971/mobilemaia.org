    Play and position analysis are available now. Saved games, full game review and more settings are planned in the browser roadmap. Electronic-board support is outside the web scope.

    Your game and study stay in this tab’s memory. Save a PGN before leaving. There are no accounts, analytics, clocks or automatic uploads. Initial engine downloads contact GitHub, jsDelivr and unpkg. Saved downloads use the benchmark’s dedicated cache.
